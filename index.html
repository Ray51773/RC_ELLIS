About RC Ellis

RC Ellis is a British indie author writing regional horror, AI and cyber comic thrillers with authentic voices and bone-dry wit. His horror fiction moves away from the usual London-centred apocalypse, asking what happens when disaster reaches the isolated South West, where tractors, fishing boats and cut-off communities become part of the fight for survival.

His newer fiction turns that same dry humour towards technology, AI and cyber security, exploring what happens when intelligent systems are given simple instructions, bad assumptions and far too much freedom. Whether the threat is undead or digital, his stories focus on ordinary people dealing with very unusual problems.
