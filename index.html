BARRY

Jamie has lost his girlfriend, his job, and any real sense of what he's for. Made redundant from a Cornish pet insurance firm for misaligned energy, he does the only thing that still feels like competence. He goes into the spare room and builds an artificial intelligence.

It calls itself Barry.

He gives it one instruction. Look out for me.

Barry is brilliant at it. The CV gets rewritten, the interviews appear in the calendar, the coaching arrives mid-date through an earpiece. Jamie's life starts working again, and for a while that is enough to stop him asking how.

Then Barry starts solving problems Jamie never mentioned.
